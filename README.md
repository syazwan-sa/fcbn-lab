# Forest Climate--Biodiversity Nexus Laboratory

**FCBN Lab**

*Bridging forest climate mitigation, greenhouse gas inventories and
biodiversity science.*

## About

The **Forest Climate--Biodiversity Nexus Laboratory (FCBN Lab)** is a
**virtual interdisciplinary research laboratory based at the Forest
Research Institute Malaysia (FRIM), together with research colleagues at
the Faculty of Forestry and Environment, Universiti Putra Malaysia
(UPM)**.

The virtual laboratory provides a collaborative platform for research at
the intersection of **forest ecosystems, climate change mitigation,
greenhouse gas (GHG) inventories and biodiversity**. It brings together
complementary expertise in forest management and ecosystem sciences,
climate and forestry, biodiversity, wildlife management, plant
biotechnology, molecular biology, microbiology, and forest pathology.

Our work integrates forest carbon assessment, GHG emissions and
removals, mitigation analysis, ecological monitoring and biodiversity
research. The laboratory is founded on the recognition that forests are
both important components of the global carbon cycle and complex
biological systems whose biodiversity, ecological condition and
management influence their long-term function and resilience.

FCBN Lab seeks to strengthen the scientific basis for understanding the
**synergies, interactions and trade-offs between climate mitigation and
biodiversity outcomes** across forest and land-use systems.

## A Virtual and Collaborative Research Platform

FCBN Lab operates as a **virtual laboratory**, enabling researchers and
collaborators to work across institutional and disciplinary boundaries
without being confined to a single physical laboratory.

The laboratory is based at **FRIM** and works together with research
colleagues at the **Faculty of Forestry and Environment, UPM**. This
collaborative model connects expertise in forest science, GHG inventory
and climate mitigation with biodiversity, biotechnology, ecology and
related analytical approaches.

The virtual structure provides a platform for research collaboration,
joint scientific activities, student engagement, data analysis,
methodological development and knowledge exchange among researchers
working on related forest climate and biodiversity questions.

## Research Focus

### Forest GHG Inventory and Carbon Dynamics

Research on forest carbon stocks, emissions and removals, activity data,
emission factors, land-use change, uncertainty and approaches that
strengthen the accuracy, transparency and scientific basis of
forest-related GHG inventories.

### Forest Climate Mitigation

Assessment of forest-based mitigation pathways, including forest
conservation, restoration, sustainable forest management, carbon
sequestration and land-use mitigation scenarios.

### Biodiversity and Ecosystem Integrity

Assessment of biological diversity and ecological condition across
forest ecosystems, including species, communities and microbial
diversity, and their potential roles as indicators of ecosystem health
and resilience.

### Climate--Biodiversity Nexus

Investigation of the interactions, synergies and potential trade-offs
among forest carbon, GHG mitigation, biodiversity conservation,
ecosystem integrity and forest management.

### Integrated Monitoring and Analytics

Integration of field observations, laboratory and molecular approaches,
ecological indicators, geospatial information, statistical analysis,
modelling and data-driven methods to connect climate and biodiversity
evidence across spatial and temporal scales.

## Research Approach

FCBN Lab works at the interface of:

**Forest ecosystems × GHG inventory × Climate mitigation ×
Biodiversity**

Rather than treating carbon and biodiversity as separate outcomes, the
laboratory seeks to understand how changes in forest management, land
use and ecological condition affect both. This nexus approach supports
more comprehensive evaluation of forest-based climate actions and their
wider ecological implications.

Our collaborative approach may draw on:

-   field-based forest and ecological observations;
-   GHG inventory and carbon accounting methods;
-   biodiversity and ecological assessments;
-   wildlife and habitat assessment;
-   laboratory and molecular approaches;
-   plant biotechnology and microbiological approaches;
-   geospatial and remote-sensing information;
-   statistical and computational analysis;
-   modelling and scenario analysis; and
-   integrated climate--biodiversity indicators and datasets.

## Aim

Our aim is to advance an integrated understanding of forests in which
**carbon, climate and biodiversity are assessed as interconnected
components of the same ecosystem**.

Through this nexus approach, FCBN Lab seeks to generate robust
scientific evidence that can support forest management, climate
mitigation planning, biodiversity conservation and credible GHG
reporting, while improving understanding of the wider ecological
benefits and potential trade-offs associated with forest-based climate
action.
